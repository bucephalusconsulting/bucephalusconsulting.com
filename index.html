What We Do

Services

Practical, expert guidance for the regulatory and planning challenges that matter most to your community.

Heritage Planning

Heritage research, policy review, designation and register support, cultural landscape analysis, and coordination of Heritage Impact Assessment work. Where a formal credential or specialist report is required, we work with appropriately qualified collaborators.

Archaeological Process Support

Archaeological process coordination and integration with planning, environmental, and heritage work. Archaeological assessment, fieldwork, reporting, and technical advice are delivered by or in collaboration with provincially licensed consultant archaeologists, as required.

Environmental Screening

Desktop environmental and species-at-risk screening, natural heritage policy review, and regulatory-pathway analysis. Where field investigations or regulated professional opinions are required, we coordinate appropriately qualified specialists.

Land Use Planning

Official Plan and Zoning By-law review, development application support, and planning policy analysis. We help municipalities and applicants navigate the planning approvals process with practical, informed guidance.

Natural Heritage Planning

Natural heritage policy screening, ecological-constraint research, and environmental-assessment coordination. We help identify potential constraints early and engage qualified field specialists where required.

Policy & Interdisciplinary Review

Official Plan conformity and regulatory review across cultural heritage, natural heritage, and environmental policy, together with independent research and hearing-preparation support for legal and planning teams.

About the Firm

Integrated heritage, environmental, and planning support.

Bucephalus Consulting helps municipalities, legal professionals, community organizations, and development teams resolve heritage, environmental, and planning questions through focused research, clear advice, and responsive project support.

Our work combines advanced academic training with practical experience in planning, environmental compliance, heritage, archaeology, GIS, research, and project coordination. We understand how requirements interact across disciplines and assemble qualified collaborators when a project calls for regulated professional work.

Our expertise spans:

  • Heritage conservation research, policy review, and Ontario Heritage Act process support
  • Archaeological process coordination with licensed consultant archaeologists
  • Environmental screening, natural heritage policy, and species-at-risk research
  • Land-use planning, Official Plan policy, and regulatory review
  • GIS analysis, mapping, and spatial research
  • Interdisciplinary research and hearing-preparation support
  • Project coordination and access to qualified specialist collaborators
